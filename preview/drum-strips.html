Drum editor ▸ Mixer strips ▸ the level meters

The meters are the two thin bars that fill from the left in each row. Before, they sat along the row's bottom edge, two hairlines thick each, and reached up into the strip's controls: the Solo disc's foot and the Pan and Level dots sat on them. Now the controls take the row's top 75 %, and the meters keep to the quarter below, thinned to fit it, with rounded ends. Drawn at the app's measurements (U = 32 px, a hairline 2 px, a row 62 px).

The Open Hat's empty meter is left as it is: the closed hat chokes it (cut group A).
