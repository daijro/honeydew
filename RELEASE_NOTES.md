Honeydew 0.5.3 makes the picker keyboard quieter, movable and optional, lets you hear Generate's preview as it plays, and centres the rack tab and keeps it from covering other buttons.

- Pickers. The keys at the bottom of instrument pickers are smaller and drawn in the theme's colours. Drag them sideways to move along the keyboard, pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom, and they light up with what the channel plays. Settings has Keyboard in pickers to turn them off.
- Generate. The preview plays while the panel is open, the clip grows to fit the bars you generate, and Play and Pause work without closing the panel.
- Rack tab. It sits in the middle of the space left between the top bar and the transport bar, and a place you drag it to keeps its share of that space when the keyboard panel changes size. Dragging the rack tab, the graph tab or the editors' close ribbon stops before it covers another button (#114).
